BNPL Credit Risk & Default Analysis

Investigated whether Buy Now Pay Later services fulfil their promise of financial empowerment or disproportionately burden vulnerable users. Analysed 10,345 synthetic transactions across 6 countries using SQL and Python to identify default risk predictors. Found that users with 3 or more missed payments defaulted at over 80% compared to 27.73% for users with no missed payments. Students and unemployed users defaulted at nearly 49%, compared to 26% for self-employed users. Analysis grounded in Canadian regulatory context (FCAC, Payments Canada).

Analysis of Cocoa Futures Market

Evaluated five forecasting models — ARIMA, ARIMAX, GARCH, VARMA, and XGBoost — on Ghanaian cocoa price data spanning January 2000 to December 2024. XGBoost outperformed all traditional time series models, achieving an MAE of 38.11 and RMSE of 235.46 compared to ARIMA models with MAE exceeding 5,000. Incorporated climate variables including precipitation and temperature alongside macroeconomic indicators to identify external drivers of price instability.

Resting Heart Rate as a Health Indicator

Built and validated a multiple linear regression model on 3,340 NHANES participants (2009-2012) to quantify the relationship between lifestyle factors and resting heart rate. Found BMI (p = 1.23e-05), sleep hours (p = 0.0003) and perceived health status to be significant predictors. Participants rating health as poor had 5.1% higher log-pulse than those rating it as good. Applied residual diagnostics and log-transformations to ensure statistical validity.
